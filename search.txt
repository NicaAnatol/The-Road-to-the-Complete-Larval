Review question
How did imaging and computational reconstruction methods enable increasingly large-scale larval Drosophila melanogaster connectomics, and how did reported dataset scale and coverage change?

Search scope
The supplied Scopus and Web of Science exports cover the planned publication-year range 2010-2026. The 2026 records are partial. The 2015-2024 tables are retrospective summaries of the existing exports, not a separate search.
The Scopus Search API query date recorded in the local log is 2026-11-04.
Web of Science was searched directly on its website. The exact query history and search date were not retained.

Boolean search blocks
The expressions below are the core Boolean queries without database field wrappers or year filters. OR combines synonyms within a concept; AND combines organism, life stage, and connectivity/method concepts.

Query 1
(drosophila OR "fruit fly" OR "fly") AND (larva* OR larval) AND (connectom* OR "wiring diagram" OR "synaptic connectivity")

Query 2
drosophila AND (larva* OR larval) AND ("electron microscopy" OR "serial section*" OR "volume EM" OR "neuron reconstruction" OR "circuit reconstruction")

Query 3
drosophila AND (larva* OR larval) AND ("neural circuit*" OR "neuronal circuit*") AND (connectom* OR "electron microscop*")

Scopus API mapping and run
The three field-neutral expressions were mapped to Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY syntax and the original publication-year filter 2010-2026. Query 1 returned 142 results, Query 2 returned 378, and Query 3 returned 57. The 577 query results include overlap and produced 488 unique Scopus records. Exact query counts are recorded in search_runs.csv.

Web of Science search and exports
The researcher searched the query blocks directly on the Web of Science website and used savedrecs (7).xls, savedrecs (8).xls, and savedrecs (9).xls. The exports contain 447 records in total. The exact WoS interface settings, query history, and search date were not retained and should be recovered or rerun before the review is complete.

Identification, date filtering, and deduplication
The supplied exports contained 935 records: 488 Scopus records and 447 WoS records. The agreed 2010-2026 date limit excluded 124 records, leaving 811 records within the period or with a year requiring verification. Deduplication removed 257 records by DOI, then normalized title and year when DOI was unavailable. One candidate pair (two records) with an exact title/year match but conflicting DOIs was retained for manual verification. The resulting title-and-abstract screening set contains 554 records. Aggregate counts are in prisma_identification_counts.csv.

Abstract handling
The 554-record screening set has 487 abstracts and 67 missing abstracts. Missing abstracts are not automatic exclusions. In the 2015-2024 Scopus subset, 269 of 340 source-export records have abstracts and 71 do not. Missing Scopus abstracts were manually looked up where accessible, as reported by the researcher. An OpenAlex enrichment check was also attempted for all 488 Scopus records, but per-record abstract provenance was not preserved; an exact manual-lookup count cannot be reported.

Screening
Title-and-abstract screening was performed in ASReview LAB across the 554 deduplicated records: 32 are currently labeled relevant, 507 excluded with recorded reasons, and 15 unclear. The 32 relevant and 15 unclear labels require full-text eligibility assessment; they are not final study-inclusion decisions. The concise files asreview_included.csv, asreview_excluded.csv, and asreview_unclear.csv contain identifiers, titles, years, DOIs, labels, and available reasons, but no abstracts. Eligibility rules are in screening_criteria.csv.

Bibliometric analysis and limits
The initial bibliometric profile uses R 4.6.2 and bibliometrix 5.5.0. Yearly output is calculated for the full 554-record set; author, affiliation, source, citation, and keyword analyses are focused on the 32 records currently labeled relevant. The initial keyword thesaurus is keyword_thesaurus.csv.
The broad search term "fly" may retrieve unrelated records and should be assessed during query refinement.
Citation counts use the maximum of the Scopus and WoS values per record rather than adding overlapping database counts. Their capture date was not retained. The merged exports do not contain cited-reference lists, so co-citation and historiographic analyses are unavailable. Affiliation data are incomplete and do not provide consistent author-to-affiliation links; institutional collaboration claims are therefore omitted. The WoS search history, citation snapshots, and screening labels remain limitations.
